# Code of Conduct

We want `pi-sprites` to be a useful, welcoming project for contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's credentials, personal information, or private communications.
- Help keep issues and pull requests focused and useful to future readers.

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

These expectations apply in this repository and in other public spaces when someone is representing the project. They complement the [Fly.io community guidelines](https://community.fly.io/faq) and [GitHub Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may limit participation when needed to protect the community. Report abusive Fly.io-related behavior privately to [`abuse@fly.io`](mailto:abuse@fly.io). Use GitHub's reporting tools for violations of GitHub's terms. Do not use a public issue to disclose private information about a conduct incident.
